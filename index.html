WHAT THIS TEST ACTUALLY DOES

Refinement, with receipts.

01 / GEOMETRY

Send a vertex once.

A coarse mesh uses original vertices. Later packets append attributes and add or remove triangles. Those early vertices stay in the final model, including skin weights and morph data.

02 / TEXTURES

Build on the pixels.

Nested pixel grids grow by receiving only their missing samples. This works across color, normal, metallic, roughness, occlusion, and emissive maps. Earlier samples remain in the final image. If this exceeds the budget, that texture uses a tiny disposable preview followed by its unchanged source image.

03 / THE BOUNDARY

A prototype, not a verdict.

Adaptive discrete geometry stages with reusable original tuples. Original animation clips arrive in the bootstrap. The encoder enforces a maximum 5% file-size overhead. Choose the meshopt + KTX2 helmet to compare against an optimized glTF input.

Benchmark conditions & current limits

Each uncached HTTP response receives its own identical server-side byte budget and initial latency. Both viewers share a tab, GPU, lighting, camera, and animation clock; decoding contention can affect results. Run repeatedly and in isolation before drawing performance conclusions. “Unrestricted” caps at 1 GiB/s; local CPU/GPU costs dominate. Sizes use decimal MB; rate controls use binary MiB/s.

The encoder accepts glTF or GLB inputs, including external resources, sparse accessors, all core primitive modes, multiple scenes, skinning and morphs. Progressive adapters cover compressed geometry, instancing, material extensions, texture transforms, variants and animation pointers. KTX2/Basis streams its original compressed mip levels after a decoded preview. Unsupported extensions are rejected explicitly. The 5% size cap remains enforced; some inputs or quality settings cannot fit it. Interactivity graphs, Gaussian splatting and diffuse transmission have progressive adapters. Unknown vendor extensions require explicit adapters.

Prior art: Hoppe, Progressive Meshes (1996). Geometry simplification uses meshoptimizer. Assets come from the Khronos sample collection.
